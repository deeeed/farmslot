- Runs waiting on a decision keep their evidence on the phone, so review opens at once on mobile data or offline: screenshots on any network; recipe videos and large images while connected over a LAN route (the app's Wi-Fi signal), as files named by their digest, verified on write and matched to their package, in the cache directory of the signed-in offline store and deleted whenever that store is (sign-out, principal change, refused credential, or eviction past the store limit), cancelling any download in flight. A file the OS reclaims is forgotten and loads again. At most 400 MB, where a newer gate displaces only older ones; a gate's media goes once it is decided. Players and image viewers read held files silently. A file's SHA-256 is computed natively off the JS thread, so a tab press never waits behind a video being checked, and the `[evidence-media]` log names each download as it starts.
