- The node no longer freezes for 10 s at a time. iOS thumbnails ran `xcrun simctl io … screenshot` through a blocking `execSync`; on a simulator that was not booted it hung until its 10 s timeout, on every 15 s thumbnail poll. Any other request in that window, such as a support-bundle cleanup or the simulator inventory, timed out behind it. Thumbnail capture, browser-pid lookup and the metrics sampler now run their commands asynchronously. A thumbnail of a simulator that is not booted is refused at once. A test keeps blocking child processes out of node request paths.
