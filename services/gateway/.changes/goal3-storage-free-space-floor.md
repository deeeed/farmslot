- Heavy gateway writes stop at a free-space floor. Every slot-to-gateway copy (completion capture into the artifact mirror, `run.refreshMirror`, session archives, the release's scaffolding archive, review workspaces), run-bundle materialization, `run.bundle.export` and `run.bundle.import`, the selected-evidence publication copy and the cross-volume run-storage archive copy refuse before writing (before their directories are created, too) when the volume has less free space than `FARMSLOT_HEAVY_WRITE_FLOOR_GB` (default 50). The error names the path, the free space and the floor. Gateway test processes default to no floor (Arthur, 11-02).
- A configured storage root on a missing volume is never recreated elsewhere. A write into a `FARMSLOT_RUNS_DIR` or `FARMSLOT_HOME` that is missing, and cannot be created in its parent because that is missing or not writable (an unmounted volume's mount point sits in root-owned `/Volumes`), is refused with "<name> <path> is missing and cannot be created in its parent (is its volume mounted?)", where `mkdir -p` would have rebuilt an unmounted volume's path on the internal disk. A configured root inside an existing, writable parent is still created on first use. The run store creates a configured `FARMSLOT_RUNS_DIR` only that way, and the run-bundle store checks both its home and the runs directory before opening for writes.
