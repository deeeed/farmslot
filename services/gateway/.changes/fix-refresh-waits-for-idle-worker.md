- `run.refreshPublishPackage` waits for an owned worker that is still making progress (a compaction, or its last turn after the completion signal), up to the 15 minutes the ready gate already waits, then refreshes. A worker at a prompt or with an unproven pane is refused at once, as before. A replay, pause or cancel of the run, or a caller that disconnects, ends the wait without a refresh. Inspection and receipt refreshes keep the immediate refusal.
