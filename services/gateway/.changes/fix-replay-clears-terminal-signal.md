- A replayed worker no longer meets the finished signal its own `mark start` refuses. Clearing a terminal `SIGNAL.json` before a worker-launch replay ran only for a native fresh restart, so on tmux transport a replay of prepare or dispatch left a `complete` or `no-change` signal in place and the new worker had no way past the start guard. Every worker-launch replay now clears it; a `running` signal is still preserved for admission to replace, and `blocked` still resumes with its checked boxes.
