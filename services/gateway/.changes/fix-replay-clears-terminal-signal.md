- A replayed worker no longer meets the finished signal its own `mark start` refuses. Clearing a finished `SIGNAL.json` before a worker-launch replay ran only for a native fresh restart, so on every other transport a replay of a step at or before dispatch left it in place and the new worker had no way past the start guard. Any such replay now clears it, and only it: each candidate is moved aside and judged on the bytes that moved, so a `running` signal a worker writes while the replay is deciding is put back rather than lost, and a `blocked` signal still resumes with its checked boxes. A snapshot is only ever put back where nothing newer exists, a snapshot left behind by an interrupted clear is recovered on the next one, and a signal that could not be restored is reported rather than passed over.
