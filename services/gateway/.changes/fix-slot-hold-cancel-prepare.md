- A run cancelled before its worker was dispatched releases its slot instead of keeping it held for replay; there is nothing to replay. The cancel first stops and joins that run's in-flight prepare, on a slot it holds or has reserved for a warm handoff. Runs cancelled after dispatch keep the replay hold (ADR-054).
- Workspace recovery, parked-work restore and parked-work retention drop their in-process prepare guard before the slot reads ready again, so a run that claims the slot right away can prepare it.
- Slot selection skips a slot still under a prepare guard. A run that asked for that slot waits for the guard, up to the same 5 minutes as a release, instead of failing with "already preparing". Branch-affinity reuse and the branch-affinity card skip a guarded slot too, and a slot that gains a guard after its card was shown is claimed only once the guard clears.
