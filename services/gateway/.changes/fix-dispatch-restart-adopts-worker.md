- A gateway restart during DISPATCH no longer stops and relaunches a tmux worker that already has its task. Dispatch records `promptDeliveryStartedAt` on disk just before the launch or keys that hand the task over (clearing it if nothing was typed), and `promptDeliveryVerifiedAt` on its step outputs once delivery is verified. On restart, a worker with that record, a live runner that took its prompt on argv, or a runner whose own hooks or signal show it took this attempt's prompt is adopted and the run resumes at MONITOR with the worker untouched. A delivery recovery can't establish blocks the run on the existing worker-left-running card with the worker and slot preserved, and the card's Replay step adopts a signal the worker has written since or dispatches again. A dispatch that never sent its prompt, or whose runner a successful read found gone with no signal, runs again; a read that timed out proves nothing and reaches the card. A dispatch attempt starts with no session recorded, so an interrupted one reopens its own. An interrupted dispatch on an absent node waits up to 120 s for it without delaying the recovery of other runs.
