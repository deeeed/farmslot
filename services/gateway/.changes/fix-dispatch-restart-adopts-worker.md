- A gateway restart during DISPATCH no longer stops and relaunches a tmux worker that already has its task. Dispatch records `promptDeliveryStartedAt` on disk just before the launch or keys that hand the task over (clearing it if nothing was typed), and `promptDeliveryVerified` on its step outputs once delivery is verified. On restart, a worker with that record, a live runner that took its prompt on argv, or a runner whose own hooks or signal show it took the prompt is adopted and the run resumes at MONITOR with the worker untouched. A delivery recovery can't establish blocks the run on the existing worker-left-running card (Replay step / Cancel run) with the worker and slot preserved. A dispatch that never sent its prompt, including one still waiting for its runner to start, or whose runner is gone with no signal, runs again.
