- The gateway refreshes its local native host at startup (then starting the current one) and on a launch refused for version skew, once no live session is busy. Before, a deploy could leave that host on the previous runtime, and every native review on the machine failed. An active run's own worker, a CI fix and a static review workspace count as busy even when idle, since their next step would need a person. Other idle sessions are stopped with their journals kept and resume on next use. `docs/operations/native-host-refresh.md` and `scripts/native-host-refresh.mts` cover the manual case.
