- A runner launch no longer asks an LLM pane classifier when its readiness wait runs out. At the deadline, an authoritative idle hook reading or the exact instruction in the live composer hands the runner to delivery, which submits a buffered instruction through its own prompt record; a launch blocker, an open question or a busy hook reading hands over nothing. Otherwise the stable-pane check gets one more readiness window, and a runner still not ready fails with its pane snapshot naming both waits.
