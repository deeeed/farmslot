- COMPLETE records where its time went on its outputs (`timing`): wall time per phase (capture and its queue, mirror, scan, selection and retain; session cost; PR lookup, push, comment, title, evidence upload, body and ready; review inputs, diff stat, the local-first package and slot release), the run-bundle lock wait and hold its captures caused, and the processes it started by kind. A release that frees a run's slot records its duration on the run (`slotRelease`): the finished-run release (COMPLETE, the deferred terminal release, ci-watch, finalize) and a `slot.release` bound to its run.
