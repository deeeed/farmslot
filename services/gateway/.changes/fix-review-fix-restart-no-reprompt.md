- A gateway restart during a review-fix round no longer breaks a worker that is mid-fix, and costs what an uninterrupted round does. Recovery reads the persisted delivery boundary (`promptDeliveryStartedAt`) and the worker's own record: a prompt acknowledged for this send, or a fix signal written at or after it, means the worker holds its prompt, so recovery sends nothing and waits for its fix signal. A recorded send nothing confirms within the launch budget blocks the run on the worker-left-running card with the worker, its slot and its mark helper preserved. The card's Replay step keeps a fix the worker holds, and an operator's Replay that again finds no proof relaunches and sends. A fix that was never sent is sent, as is one whose runner a clean read found gone; a read that failed or timed out proves nothing. A relaunch clears the replaced attempt's signal, so a leading worker clock cannot confirm the new send. A recovered pipeline fix reads the snapshot its review persisted, so a small fix is accepted without another reviewer, and a replayed REVIEW keeps the review's validation depth. Replay step works on every REVIEW card, with an output review open or the worker gone.
