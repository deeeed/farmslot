- A gateway restart during a review-fix round no longer breaks a worker that is mid-fix. Recovery reads the persisted delivery boundary (`promptDeliveryStartedAt`) and the attempt the worker's own signal bound (`signalAttemptId`): a worker holding its prompt is left alone and recovery waits for its fix signal, a send nothing can confirm takes the prompt-delivery-uncertain hold with the worker and slot preserved, and a fix that was never sent is sent. A relaunch that carries the prompt on argv now records the boundary before the respawn; one that delivers after launch clears it, since the new process holds nothing yet.
