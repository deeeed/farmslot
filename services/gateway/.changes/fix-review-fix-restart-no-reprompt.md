- A gateway restart during a review-fix round no longer breaks a worker that is mid-fix. Recovery reads the persisted delivery boundary (`promptDeliveryStartedAt`) and the worker's own record: a prompt acknowledged for this send, or a fix signal written at or after it, means the worker holds its prompt, so recovery sends nothing and waits for its fix signal. A recorded send nothing confirms within the launch budget blocks the run on the worker-left-running card with the worker, its slot and its mark helper preserved, and the card's Replay step re-checks without sending again. A fix that was never sent is sent, as is one whose runner a clean read found gone; a read that failed or timed out proves nothing. A relaunch that carries the prompt on argv records a new boundary before the respawn, so an earlier worker's signal cannot confirm it; one that delivers after launch clears the boundary once the new process replaced the old.
