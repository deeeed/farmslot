# Runner capacity leverage

## Product promise

Turn multiple model runners, computers, and subscriptions into coordinated engineering capacity instead of disconnected chat windows.

## Narration script

> Most engineers already have access to more than one model runner or subscription. Farmslot turns that scattered capacity into a coordinated fleet. Different agents can work, review, validate, or investigate in parallel, while the operator keeps one control plane and one evidence standard. That is how one person starts operating like a team of teams.

## Shot list

1. Show multiple workers or slots available in Command Center.
2. Show different runners assigned to different jobs or review roles.
3. Show one operator view collecting their progress and evidence.
4. End with visual validation, recipe evidence, or review artifacts proving the work is not just parallel, but trustworthy.

## On-screen captions

- “Use more than one runner without losing control.”
- “Parallel work, shared evidence standard.”
- “One operator can coordinate a team of teams.”

## Success criteria

The viewer understands that Farmslot scales model capacity into managed engineering throughput, not just more simultaneous prompts.
