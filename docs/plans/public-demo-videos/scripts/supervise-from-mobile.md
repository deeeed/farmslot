# Supervise from mobile

## Product promise

Keep the approval and monitoring loop available when the operator steps away from the workstation.

## Narration script

> I do not need to sit at the workstation the whole time. Companion gives me active runs, workers, terminals, and approval states from mobile, so I can keep agents moving without giving up control.

## Shot list

1. Open Companion on sanitized fixture data.
2. Show active run or worker status.
3. Open terminal/watchlist or run detail.
4. End on the human approval or supervision state.

## On-screen captions

- “Supervise away from the desk.”
- “Workers, runs, and terminals stay visible.”
- “The human approval loop remains intact.”

## Success criteria

The viewer understands that Companion extends supervision without turning the system into unattended automation.
