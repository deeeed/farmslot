# ADR-025: Run Family Observability

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-04-15  
**Relates to:** [ADR-019](019-recipe-graph-visualization.md), [ADR-024](024-run-lanes-and-run-family-model.md)

## Context

Farmslot already captures much of the evidence needed to understand a run after the fact, but that evidence is fragmented across live-run surfaces instead of being organized around the run family:

- `run-detail` is optimized for a single active run: pipeline state, decisions, slot links, and gate handling.
- `step-inspector` exposes step inputs, outputs, artifacts, and issues, but only one step at a time.
- `ready-workspace` already shows the kinds of retrospective signals operators care about — diff, recipe, artifacts, CI, and worker learnings — but only inside a completion gate workspace.
- ADR-019 already established the recipe graph as the canonical visual representation of recipe quality.
- ADR-024 already established the run family as the canonical identity model for related runs and follow-up flows.

That leaves a product gap: there is no canonical retrospective destination that answers, quickly and reliably, all of the questions an operator asks after a family finishes:

1. What happened across the family?
2. What evidence exists?
3. What changed?
4. Was the recipe good?
5. What did we learn at each step or follow-up?

The current live-run pages are useful for in-flight triage, but they are not the right canonical surface for retrospective observability. They bury family context behind run-local navigation, spread evidence across workspaces, and do not guarantee a normalized learnings/evidence model across follow-up runs such as `review-pr` and `pr-complete`.

## Decision

Adopt a dedicated **family observability** model with its own fullscreen retrospective surface.

### 1. Canonical destination: dedicated fullscreen family view

The canonical place to revisit past work is a dedicated fullscreen **family observability** view, organized around the ADR-024 run-family model rather than a single run.

Other surfaces may link into this destination, but they do not replace it.

### 2. Operational vs retrospective separation

`run-detail` remains the operational single-run page for live status, decisions, and drill-down.

The new family observability view becomes the retrospective surface for completed or historical understanding. This separation avoids overloading `run-detail` with both operational and retrospective responsibilities.

### 3. Evidence-before-narration data model

The product must persist enough normalized family metadata and artifacts to answer the retrospective questions directly, before any LLM synthesis is attempted.

That snapshot must be reconstructable for past families from persisted run/completion artifacts and metadata alone; it must not depend on a still-live slot, terminal session, or other ephemeral runtime state.

The v1 family snapshot must make room for, at minimum:

- family summary / family identity
- ordered runs and timeline context
- explicit evidence/media references
- diff/change summary
- recipe graph / recipe quality inputs
- family-level learnings aggregated from worker learnings, self-review signals, and follow-up completion metadata
- explicit missing-data markers when evidence is absent

### 4. Reuse existing primitives instead of inventing parallel ones

The family observability view should compose existing primitives where practical:

- ADR-019 recipe graph for recipe visualization
- ADR-024 family identity and inherited context model
- existing pipeline/step-inspector interaction patterns for timeline + step drill-down
- existing ready-workspace evidence/diff/learnings concepts where they fit a retrospective view

This ADR creates a new canonical surface, not a parallel design language.

### 5. Family reports are generated on demand

A full family report may be generated by the gateway LLM or a side agent, but it is **not** a required persisted artifact for every completed family in v1.

The report is derived from the normalized family snapshot, with raw-data fallback when synthesis is unavailable or incomplete.

### 6. First-screen comprehension is part of the contract

The initial family observability viewport should surface the highest-value retrospective signals immediately: summary, evidence/screenshots, diff/change context, and recipe quality.

Operators should not need to navigate through run-local tabs or hidden panels just to answer the core “what happened and was it good?” questions.

## Decision Drivers

- Operators need a dedicated retrospective destination, not just a richer single-run inspector.
- Evidence, screenshots, diff context, recipe quality, and learnings must be understandable in under ~30 seconds.
- Review and completion follow-ups should contribute learnings to the same self-improvement loop instead of living as isolated artifacts.
- Existing ADRs already provide the right family and recipe foundations; this work should leverage them rather than replace them.

## Alternatives Considered

### A. Expand `run-detail` into the canonical observability destination

**Rejected.**

This is the cheapest route structurally, but it keeps retrospective understanding tied to a page that is already optimized for live operations and single-run workflow resolution.

### B. Make the generated family report the primary UX

**Rejected.**

That puts narration ahead of evidence and makes observability quality depend too heavily on synthesis quality. Operators need the underlying evidence, diff context, recipe quality, and learnings even when report generation fails.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Past work becomes family-first and easier to revisit.
- Recipe quality stays visually grounded in ADR-019 instead of falling back to raw JSON.
- Follow-up runs such as review and completion can feed one shared retrospective learnings model.
- Future observability features gain one canonical surface instead of several ad hoc ones.

### Negative

- Requires a new protocol/gateway snapshot model plus a new fullscreen UI route.
- Requires more disciplined learnings/evidence normalization across follow-up steps.
- Introduces another top-level retrospective destination that must stay clearly distinct from live operational pages.

## Follow-ups

The following are intentionally deferred from v1 and stay on the roadmap as follow-on work:

1. review artifact upload plus PR comment screenshot/video tables
2. apps/companion/mobile entry points into family observability
3. human grading relocation or reframe inside the observability context
