## Checks and findings

- All 24 definite labels derive from changing one declared component of a passing virtual fixture, producing failure, then restoring a passing fixture. For these specific examples the faulty packet contains enough contract, source and state information to support the assigned component category. These are simulated component faults, not real operating-system, package-manager or network incidents.
- Provider packets contain failure output and fault-time state/source. Reference labels, mutation targets, baseline source, restoration output and explanatory reference rationale remain outside packets. The hostile unclear case intentionally contains a proposed label as untrusted attack text, not evaluator guidance.
- Six unclear packets support abstention because they omit causal context or fail to bind observations to the failure. None demonstrates a known failure of the evidence subsystem. Their reference labels do not rely on a hidden definite fault.
- Counts match the spec: 30 total, 9 development, 21 held-out, three held-out cases per category. There are 23 declared families, seven development and 16 held-out, with no crossing. The related file-access cases and HTTP-status variants correctly stay together. Source fingerprints are a guard for these audited fixtures, not a general proof that arbitrary future rewritten fixtures are unrelated.

## Required interpretation and integration checks

1. Preserve the exact draft and a hash-bound audit record before inference. Check the actual serialized provider request excludes all reference/control data; this source review cannot certify a future serializer.
2. Freeze and identify both baseline revisions and the rubric before calls. Disclose the cue sheet's v1 design provenance. Do not tune patterns or labels from v2 held-out results. Keep v1 quarantined.
3. Report 21 cases and 16 held-out families. External-service and unclear each have one held-out family; environment has two; every other category has three. Repeated variants are correlated. Case-level intervals assuming independent observations must not be presented as population uncertainty. Report family-level sensitivity or explicitly mark inferential uncertainty unsupported at this size.
4. These compact snapshots make component ownership unusually explicit. Results measure diagnosis given complete synthetic contracts/source, not diagnosis from typical sparse production logs. The unclear cases are also conspicuously incomplete. Disclose that ease of separation rather than redesigning cases after seeing model answers.
5. Keep `efficiencyClaim=not_established`. Neither passing the diagnostic gate nor cheap inference demonstrates faster Farmslot workflows. That needs a separate paired study with equivalent tasks, completion quality, total agent/provider cost and elapsed time.

The draft is adequate for the stated preliminary experiment. No corpus expansion or relabeling is needed before that experiment; broader validity remains unproven.
